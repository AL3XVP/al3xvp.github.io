Recallo

Study app for nursing students

Flashcards and exam prep for mental health and maternity nursing courses: 900+ cards and 1,000+ NCLEX-style questions. Reviews are scheduled with spaced repetition, and a mock exam can be generated on demand, followed by a breakdown of strong and weak topics. It installs on a phone and works offline, and the question types go past multiple choice to select-all, ordering, and case studies.
